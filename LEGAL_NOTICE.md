The Universal Fairness Protocol (UFP) is a modular, self-hosted framework for verifiable fairness systems.

By default:
- UFP collects only a **username** and **password** during user registration.
- No real names, email addresses, phone numbers, or other personal identifiers are collected.
- UFP does not process payments, store financial information, or use tracking cookies or analytics systems.

The core system is designed solely to demonstrate cryptographic fairness logic based on SHA-256 hashing, segment extraction, and verifiable outcomes.
